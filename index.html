Riya Bhattacharya, PhD

Research Associate & Adjunct Faculty, Department of Public Policy
M.S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences, Bengaluru

I work on urban governance, urbanisation, migration and urban planning, with a focus on decentralisation and development. My research uses both quantitative and qualitative methods to study how citizens participate in local governance and how cities deliver basic services.

I received my PhD in Development Studies (University of Mysore) in August 2026 from the Centre for Decentralisation and Development, Institute for Social and Economic Change (ISEC), Bengaluru, under the supervision of Prof. D. Rajasekhar. My thesis, “Decentralised Governance, Participation and Service Delivery: A Study of Extended Metropolitan Kolkata,” evaluates how the 74th Constitutional Amendment has translated into service delivery and citizen participation in suburban municipalities, combining household surveys, regression analysis and geospatial methods. I am also Adjunct Faculty in the Department of Public Policy, where I teach postgraduate courses in urban governance and quantitative methods and lead students in field-based GIS research.
